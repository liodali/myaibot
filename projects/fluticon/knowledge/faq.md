- Support hours: _e.g. Mon–Fri, 09:00–18:00 CET_
- Languages: English, Arabic, French

## How it works

- Font generation runs **locally on your device** — your icons don't need to
  leave your machine to build a font.
- Import SVGs, preview the icon set, export an OpenType font plus the Flutter
  `IconData` class.
- PNG images can be converted to SVG before adding them to a set.
- Paid plans add cloud project sync and snapshots, so projects follow you
  across devices.
- Signing in on a new device requires **device approval** — it's a security
  feature; check your existing device or email to approve.

## Common questions

### Do my icons get uploaded anywhere?

No — font generation happens locally on your device. Cloud sync is optional
and only on paid plans (Plus and above).

### How do I use PNG images as icons?

Convert them first: the PNG-to-SVG tool turns them into SVGs, then add them
to your icon set like any other SVG.

### How do I sync projects across devices?

Upgrade to Plus or Pro — cloud project sync keeps your sets in sync and
saves cloud snapshots.

### Why is my new device asking for approval?

Security: each new device must be approved before it can access your
account. Approve it from an already-trusted device or the emailed link.

### How do I cancel or get a refund?

Billing runs through the app store / payment provider. Cancellations take
effect at the end of the billing period. For refunds, escalate to a human.
